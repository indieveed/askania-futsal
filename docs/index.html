How to subscribe

Google Calendar
  1. Open Google Calendar on the web.
  2. Next to Other calendars, choose + → From URL.
  3. Paste the feed URL and choose Add calendar.
Outlook
  1. Open Outlook Calendar.
  2. Choose Add calendar → Subscribe from web.
  3. Paste the feed URL and confirm.
Other calendar apps

Look for Subscribe by URL, Add calendar from URL, iCalendar subscription, or Web calendar, and use the feed URL above.
